Bird Care Guide

Birds are intelligent, social and long-lived — their needs go well beyond just food and a cage.

Cage & Space

Choose the largest cage you can fit, with horizontal bars for climbing species like parrots. Birds need room to fully spread and flap their wings without touching the sides.

Diet

A pellet-based diet supplemented with fresh vegetables and limited fruit is healthier than a seed-only diet, which is often too high in fat. Avoid avocado, chocolate and caffeine — all toxic to birds.

Mental Stimulation

Birds are highly intelligent and prone to boredom. Rotate toys regularly, provide foraging opportunities, and spend daily one-on-one time with social species like conures and parrots.

Health Signs to Watch

  • Fluffed-up feathers for long periods
  • Changes in droppings
  • Reduced vocalisation or activity
  • Tail-bobbing while breathing
