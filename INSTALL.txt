VIMAL MOBILE DROPDOWN ONLY - ADD-ON

IMPORTANT:
- This does NOT replace navbar.js.
- This does NOT replace navbar.css.
- Desktop navbar/dropdowns are NOT changed.
- It only changes the MOBILE slide-out menu at max-width: 768px.

1. Copy:
   css/vimal-mobile-dropdown-addon.css
   into your existing Vimal-Aqua/css/ folder.

2. Copy:
   js/vimal-mobile-dropdown-addon.js
   into your existing Vimal-Aqua/js/ folder.

3. In each HTML page that loads the navbar, AFTER your existing:
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="css/navbar.css?v=2">

   add:
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="css/vimal-mobile-dropdown-addon.css?v=1">

4. AFTER your existing:
   <script src="js/navbar.js?v=32"></script>

   add:
   <script src="js/vimal-mobile-dropdown-addon.js?v=1"></script>

Do not remove or replace your existing navbar files.

Desktop stays exactly as your current navbar.
On phones, the hamburger opens grouped dropdowns for Toys, Pets & Accessories, and Become a Partner.
