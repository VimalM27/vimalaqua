VIMAL HOME PAGE OFFER POPUP
===========================

1. Copy into your project:
     css/vimal-home-offer-popup.css
     js/vimal-home-offer-popup.js

2. In index.html ONLY, add these two lines:

   In <head>, right after the app-popup stylesheet line:
     <link rel="stylesheet" href="css/vimal-home-offer-popup.css?v=1">

   At the bottom, right after the app-popup script line:
     <script src="js/vimal-home-offer-popup.js?v=1"></script>

3. Open js/vimal-home-offer-popup.js and edit the block marked "EDIT HERE":
   offer text, big number (20%), coupon code, start/end dates, buttons, WhatsApp number.
   (The text in there is SAMPLE content. Replace it with your real offer.)

HOW IT BEHAVES
 - Home page only. Order: welcome popup -> app popup -> offer popup. It waits until the
   others are closed, so they never stack.
 - Shows once every 24 hours per visitor (change FREQUENCY to "session" or "always").
 - After the "end" date it stops showing by itself. The countdown follows the same date.
 - Close with X, "No thanks", Escape, or tapping outside.
 - Test any time, ignoring dates and the once-a-day rule:  yourwebsite/index.html?offerPopup=1
 - To switch it off: set enabled: false.

NOTE: your site has no coupon/discount system at checkout yet. The code is shown for the
customer to quote on WhatsApp, by phone or in the store. Tell me if you want the code to
apply automatically in the cart.
