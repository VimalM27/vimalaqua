STEP 1 - Database (do this first, once)
  Supabase > SQL Editor > paste supabase-coupons.sql > Run.
  (Run supabase-orders.sql first if you have not already; it is already in your project.)
  This creates the coupon table, the discount columns on orders, and the 7 festive coupons.

STEP 2 - Copy these files into your Vimal-Aqua folder (same paths, replace when asked)
  NEW   offers.html, faq.html, css/offers.css, css/faq.css, supabase-coupons.sql
  EDIT  js/order-checkout.js   coupon box + discounted total + UPI QR amount at checkout
        js/my-orders.js        shows the coupon line on the customer's order
        js/admin-orders.js     shows the coupon on the admin order ("amount to match" is the discounted total)
        js/bill.js             coupon line on the bill, GST worked out on the discounted amount
        cart.html              small "have a coupon?" hint
        js/navbar.js           Offers link + Contact dropdown (Contact Us / FAQ)
        css/vimal-dropdown-unified.css, sitemap.xml

Changing or adding offers
  1. Edit/add the coupon row in Supabase (table vp_coupons, or the insert list at the bottom of supabase-coupons.sql).
  2. Edit the OFFERS list in offers.html so the page shows the same rules.
  The database is what actually decides the discount; the page is only the display.
