VIMAL PETS & TOYS - HOME PAGE APP DOWNLOAD POPUP (boy & girl + phone)
======================================================================
Shows ONLY on the home page. Nothing else on your site is changed.

STEP 1 - Copy the 3 files into your existing project (same folders):
   css/vimal-home-app-popup.css
   js/vimal-home-app-popup.js
   images/app-popup-kids.jpg

STEP 2 - Edit ONLY index.html and add 2 lines:

  (a) in <head>, right after this existing line:
        <link rel="stylesheet" href="css/vimal27-enhancements.css">
      add:
        <link rel="stylesheet" href="css/vimal-home-app-popup.css?v=1">

  (b) near the bottom, right AFTER this existing line:
        <script src="js/navbar.js?v=32"></script>
      add:
        <script src="js/vimal-home-app-popup.js?v=1"></script>

   (It must come AFTER navbar.js. That is how it replaces the older
    popup on the home page so two popups never appear together.)

STEP 3 - Put your real download link
   Open js/vimal-home-app-popup.js and change APP_URL at the top.
   Default is  https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vimal27.petsworld
   (works once the app is live on Play Store). Until then you can use
   the direct .apk link instead.

TEXT, DELAY and "how many days to hide after closing" are all in the
EDIT HERE block at the top of the js file.

TESTING
   - Once someone closes it, it stays hidden for 3 days (per browser).
     To see it again any time, open:  https://www.vimal27.in/?appPopup=1
     (or index.html?appPopup=1)
   - It is skipped for iPhone/iPad (the app is Android only) and for people
     already inside the installed app.
   - Hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) after uploading.
