OUR STORY

More Than a Store.
A Passion That Connects.

Vimal Pets & Toys was born from a genuine passion for animals, a love for quality and a vision to create a destination where pet lovers and families can discover products they value.

Bringing together pets, pet essentials, aquariums, accessories and toys for children, we aim to make discovering the right products a convenient and enjoyable experience.

We believe that every interaction matters — from discovering a new product to finding the right essentials for the ones you care about.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE BRAND

Meet Vimal. The Passion Behind the Brand.

Driven by a lifelong passion for animals, Vimal is a pet enthusiast, wildlife rescuer and snake rescue expert dedicated to making a difference for both pets and wildlife.

Through Vimal Pets & Toys, he brings together quality pet supplies, practical knowledge and a growing community of pet lovers and families.

Beyond business, his involvement in wildlife rescue and awareness reflects his belief in protecting nature and encouraging safe and respectful coexistence between people and wildlife.

Passion. Purpose. Responsibility.
