Through Vimal Pets World, he brings together quality pets, trusted pet supplies, expert advice, and practical knowledge to help pet lovers provide the best care.

Beyond pets, he actively rescues and conserves snakes while spreading awareness to encourage safe and respectful coexistence between people and wildlife.
