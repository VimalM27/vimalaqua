Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery, pets, aquariums and returns.

Ordering & Payments

How do I place an order?
Add the items you want to your cart, open the cart and follow the checkout steps. You can also call or WhatsApp us on +91 78 4545 2727 and we will help you place the order.
How can I cancel an order?
You can cancel before the order is dispatched. Call or WhatsApp +91 78 4545 2727, or email vimal@vimal27.in with your order details. Once a live animal has been prepared for delivery, cancellation may not be possible. See the Cancellation Policy.
Where can I check my order status?
Sign in and open My Orders. You will also get a call or message once your order is out for delivery.

Delivery

Which areas do you deliver to?
We deliver within Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, and to select areas outside the city on request. Contact us to check if we can reach your location.
How long does delivery take?
Most orders arrive within 2–5 business days, depending on your location and the product type. Shipping charges, if any, are shown at checkout before you confirm. See the Shipping Policy.
How are live pets and fish delivered?
They travel in secure, ventilated packaging suited to the species, and delivery is scheduled to keep travel time and stress low.

Pets & Aquariums

Are your pets healthy?
Every pet is checked for general health and condition before dispatch. If an animal arrives unwell, contact us within 24 hours.
Can I get help setting up a new aquarium?
Yes. Read the aquarium cycling guide and the tank maintenance guide, or contact us and we will guide you through setup.
Will you help me care for my pet after I buy it?
Yes. Our team is available for care guidance after your pet comes home. Start with the Pet Care Guide or message us on WhatsApp.
Do I need to check any rules before buying a pet?
Some species are regulated locally or nationally, and it is the buyer's responsibility to make sure they can legally keep them. We also ask buyers to confirm they can provide proper care, housing and time. See the Pet Purchase Policy.

Returns & Refunds

Can I return a product?
Unopened, unused accessories and food can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
Can I return a live pet or fish?
Live animals are generally not eligible for return once delivered, because of animal welfare and health. If one arrives unwell, contact us within 24 hours.
What if my item is damaged or wrong?
Send us photos within 48 hours of delivery and we will sort it out. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method in 5–7 business days. See the Return & Refund Policy.

Partners

How do I sell on Vimal Pets & Toys?
Create an account on the Partner Signup page. Once approved, you can manage listings from your Partner Dashboard. Please read the Seller Terms first.
